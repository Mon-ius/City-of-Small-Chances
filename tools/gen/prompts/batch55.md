# Batch 55 Prompts

Key colour: GREEN #00ff00 for all three (weathered cork, faded red-and-white paint, tarred
rope, rusted iron, silvered timber — no green anywhere on any subject; the ladder's weed is
dark olive-brown, kept muted so it keys cleanly).

Driven one-image-per-`codex exec` invocation (reads-forbidden), three parallel attempts per
prop, and harvested the chosen raw locally — because codex otherwise burns its whole turn
`sed`-reading the 440-line imagegen SKILL.md before ever calling `image_gen`. Each invocation
was wrapped:

> You already know the image_gen skill — do NOT read SKILL.md or any file, do NOT inspect
> references, begin the image_gen call immediately. Generate exactly ONE image with image_gen
> (<size> size to suit), prompt: <P> . That is the only thing to do; after the image is
> generated you are done.

All nine attempts produced a PNG; the cleanest per prop was chosen from a contact sheet
(life-ring = clearest red-white quarters on the backing-board with visible grab-line beckets;
ladder = cleanest curved grab-hoops and proportions; fenders = uniform tarred woven-rope with
no olive cast to complicate the green key).
