# Batch 12 prompts

Built-in `image_gen` / GPT-Image-2 was used for all sources. Each source was generated as a 1024x1024 centered prop on a flat `#00ff00` chroma-key background, then copied to `tools/gen/source_batch12/`.

Shared constraints for every prompt:
- Warm, slightly-painterly stylised realism for City of Small Chances.
- One centered object, generous padding, no cropping, no busy scene.
- Perfectly flat solid `#00ff00` background, no gradients, texture, shadows, reflections, or floor plane.
- Absolutely no readable text, real words, numbers, brands, logos, symbols, watermarks, barcodes, currency marks, or app icons.
- Document marks are abstract greeked glyph rows, wavy ink lines, blank strokes, ticks, blocks, and boxes only.

## PROP_Eco_Receipt

A curled paper till-receipt strip, warm off-white paper, faint abstract greeked glyph-rows made only of wavy ink marks and short meaningless strokes, torn bottom edge. Face-on / gentle three-quarter framing. No cast shadow, no contact shadow, no reflection.

## PROP_Eco_BillNotice

An opened utility/rent bill prop: an envelope behind a folded slip, officious mood, off-white paper, with one bold red/maroon flat stamp block in a corner that contains no letters or symbols. Any paper markings are abstract greeked glyph rows, wavy ink lines, blank ruled strokes, and simple blocks only. No cast shadow, no contact shadow, no reflection.

## PROP_Eco_RouteCard

A transit travel-pass card, rounded-corner plastic, cool blue-grey body, abstract wayfinding chevron and arc motif, one small chip square. The card has abstract geometric motifs only. No cast shadow, no contact shadow, no reflection.

## PROP_Eco_RentNotice

A formal rent-increase / payment-reminder letter, cream paper, abstract crest block at the top, greeked body lines, one corner slightly dog-eared. Letterhead and body use abstract greeked glyph rows, wavy ink lines, blank strokes, and simple blocks only. No cast shadow, no contact shadow, no reflection.

## PROP_Eco_ApplicationForm

A clipboard holding an application form: dark board, pale form sheet, ruled blank fields and tick-box rows made of boxes only, a simple pen resting diagonally across it. Form content is blank ruled lines, abstract wavy glyph rows, and empty boxes only. No cast shadow, no contact shadow, no reflection.

Manifest content is abstract checklist grid, wavy ink rows, ticks, and blank blocks only. No cast shadow, no contact shadow, no reflection.

## PROP_Kit_Phone

A basic modern phone, face-on, simple rounded body, dark glass front with a plain warm home-screen glow. The screen is a plain warm glow with no interface details. No cast shadow, no contact shadow, no reflection.
